# Northwind Labs — Remote Work Policy (v3, updated March 2026)

## Who is eligible

All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day onboarding period may work remotely up to four days per week. Roles tagged as onsite-essential (IT support, office operations) are eligible for two remote days per week.

## Core hours

Everyone is expected to be online and reachable from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Central, Monday through Thursday. Outside core hours you set your own schedule as long as your team agrees and deadlines are met. Fridays are meeting-free focus days unless there is a customer escalation.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, monitor, keyboard, and a $750 home-office stipend every two years. Keep your devices patched — the security team pushes required updates on the first Tuesday of each month, and you must reboot within 48 hours.

## Communication

Default to async: write it in Slack or a doc before scheduling a call. Cameras on for 1:1s and interviews, optional otherwise. If you will be offline for more than two hours during core hours, drop a note in your team channel.

## Time off

Submit PTO requests in BambooHR at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days. The company observes 12 holidays per year plus a week-long winter break from December 24 through January 1.
