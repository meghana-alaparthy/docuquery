Pulseboard FAQ

What is Pulseboard?
Pulseboard is a lightweight analytics dashboard for small teams. Connect your database or upload a CSV and get shareable charts in minutes, without writing SQL.

How much does it cost?
There is a free Starter plan (3 dashboards, 1,000 rows per dataset). The Team plan is $19 per user per month with unlimited dashboards and 10M rows per dataset. Annual billing saves 20%.

Can I share dashboards outside my team?
Yes. Every dashboard has a public link option with optional password protection. Viewers do not need a Pulseboard account.

Which databases are supported?
Postgres, MySQL, BigQuery, Snowflake, and SQLite. CSV and Parquet uploads work on every plan.

Is my data secure?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest (AES-256). Pulseboard is SOC 2 Type II certified. We never train models on customer data.

How do I cancel?
Cancel anytime from Settings > Billing. You keep access until the end of your billing period and can export all dashboards as PDF.
